How we protect data

Data stays in the EU

Servers and backups in Frankfurt.

Encryption

With a key held in the EU; every use of the key is logged.

Two-factor sign-in

For every account at the clinic, no exceptions.

Data processing agreement

The clinic remains the controller of its clients' data; we process it only on the clinic's instructions.
